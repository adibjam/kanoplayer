Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 29, 2026

Thank you for using Kano Player.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Kano Player handles information when you use the application.

About Kano Player

Kano Player is a media player that loads and plays content referenced by KANO configuration files. The application does not host, publish, upload, or distribute any media content.

Information We Collect

Kano Player does not require user registration and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or account credentials.

The application does not operate any servers for collecting or storing user data.

KANO Files

KANO configuration files are created and distributed by users or third parties.

When you import a KANO file, all processing is performed locally on your device.

If a KANO file is password protected, the password is used only on your device to decrypt the file. Passwords are never transmitted to the developer or any external server.

Device Permissions

Kano Player only requests the permissions required for its core functionality.

Currently, the application does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or other sensitive personal information.

Internet access is required only to retrieve media or configuration files referenced by KANO files.

Advertising

Kano Player displays advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners, including:

*   [AdMob](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
*   [Appodeal](https://appodeal.com/privacy-policy)

These services may collect device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and other technical information as described in their own privacy policies for the purpose of delivering advertisements.

Kano Player does not control the data collection practices of these third-party advertising providers.

Third-Party Content

The application only plays content referenced by KANO configuration files.

The developer does not create, review, host, monitor, or control third-party content, external websites, or media links contained in KANO files.

Users are solely responsible for the KANO files they import and the content they access through the application.

Data Security

All KANO file processing and decryption are performed locally on your device.

The developer does not receive or store your KANO files, passwords, or media history.

Children's Privacy

Kano Player is not specifically intended for children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes become effective when the updated version is published.
